Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

Übel is a desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. The people who make Übel never receive, store or see your data. There is no Übel server.

Google data

If you choose to connect a Google account, Übel asks for access to Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks so it can show your email, events and reminders and act on them when you ask (for example archiving an email, saving a draft, sending a reply you approved, adding an event, or syncing a reminder).

  • Your Google data travels directly between Google and your computer. It is not sent to the makers of Übel or to any other server.
  • Übel's sign-in token is stored in your computer's secure keychain (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain).
  • Übel does not sell, share or transfer Google data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Google data is not used to train AI models.

Übel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features (optional)

If you add your own Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) API key, the messages you type into Übel's chat are sent to that provider to get an answer, under that provider's terms. Google data is not sent to AI providers unless you paste it into the chat yourself.

Files on your computer

Übel only looks at folders you choose to add, and only moves files when a rule you set or your approval says so. It never deletes your files. Settings, reminders and history are stored in Übel's folder on your computer.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google in Übel's Settings, or remove Übel's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Übel and deleting its app-data folder removes everything it stored.
